Why Generic Medicines Matter: Affordable Healthcare for All

25 May 2026 • Generic Medicines

Generic medicines play a crucial role in making healthcare affordable and accessible for millions of people in India. They offer the same quality, safety, and efficacy as branded medicines but at a fraction of the cost.

What Are Generic Medicines?

Generic medicines are pharmaceutical drugs that contain the same active ingredients as their branded counterparts. They are approved by regulatory authorities and must meet strict quality standards.

Benefits of Generic Medicines

  • Affordability: Generics are significantly less expensive, making treatment accessible to a larger population.
  • Quality: All generics must meet the same quality and safety standards as branded medicines.
  • Availability: A wide range of generic medicines is available for most common and chronic conditions.

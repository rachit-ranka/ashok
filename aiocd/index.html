Why it's accurate

It handles the messy real-world details

Verified against real invoices — the taxable total reconciles to the invoice, to the paisa.

Multi-page invoices

Any number of items across any number of pages, including repeating or missing headers.

Mixed GST slabs

5%, 12%, 18% on the same invoice — each line gets its correct rate and net taxable.

Box → strip conversion

Reads the pack (e.g. 10X10) and converts box quantities and rates to strips automatically.

Discounts & free goods

Value discounts and scheme stock are split into billed + free, matching how you record it.

Exact rate precision

Per-strip rates keep full precision — no silent rounding that throws your totals off.

You stay in control

Every field lands in an editable grid, so you verify and adjust before anything is generated.
